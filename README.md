# Datheon-Internship
## Task 1 – Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA)

**Dataset:** Superstore Sales

Performed exploratory data analysis using Python to identify sales trends, customer behaviour, product performance, regional patterns and profitability.

**Tools Used:** Python, Pandas, Matplotlib, Seaborn, Google Colab

**Key Highlights:**
- Analyzed customer segments, product categories and regional sales.
- Identified monthly sales trends and top-performing products.
- Evaluated profitability and loss-making products.
- Studied the relationship between discounts and average profit.

**Key Insights:**
- Technology was the highest-selling product category.
- The West region recorded the highest sales and profit.
- June recorded the highest monthly sales.
- A 50% discount level showed negative average profit.
